# Privacy Policy — Reels Publisher

Last updated: October 19, 2026

## Introduction

Reels Publisher is a personal tool designed to help its operator publish
video content to their Instagram account.

## Information We Access

The application may access Instagram account identifiers, account
permissions, and video content necessary to publish Reels.

The application may use access tokens to authenticate with Meta's APIs.
Access tokens are sensitive credentials and should not be shared.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate with Instagram and publish
video content requested by the application operator.

## Storage and Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell personal information.
Information is processed only as necessary to operate the application
and communicate with Meta's services.

Access tokens may be stored locally on the operator's computer.

## Data Deletion

To request deletion of information associated with this application,
contact the application operator using the contact information provided
with the application.
